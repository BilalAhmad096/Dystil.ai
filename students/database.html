Test an email

Sends one copy to one person on the team. Tests are never recorded, so the same email can be checked as often as you like and nobody on the real list loses their place.

Reminders

Each reminder goes to everyone who has registered, once. Both names write to the same record, so sending “2 days to go” from Frank means the Dystil button skips those people rather than mailing them again. Sends go one at a time, so allow around twelve minutes.
